# Country flag assets

These SVG national flags are sourced unchanged from [lipis/flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), commit `ac7e0eb3259eb62aa6100c435607e2fe03896a97`, `flags/4x3/*.svg`.

License: MIT; see [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt). Flag symbols and their use may also be subject to local law and sporting-body representation requirements. KVL uses the pre-existing verified `flag_iso2` fields from `data/international/country-directory-v1.json`, without inferring country identities.

The locally stored assets support offline-compatible GitHub Pages rendering and eliminate live dependencies on third-party flag CDNs.
